02 — ABOUT

Restoring Purpose, Security, and Dignity

Timeless Vision Initiative is a registered Nigerian foundation addressing the mental, emotional, and financial well-being of older adults.

Official Status: CAC Registered RC 7164522

Our Mission

To improve the quality of life for older adults by providing a supportive community that promotes physical and mental health, financial independence, emotional well-being, and social connection.

Our Vision

To create a society where older adults live independently with purpose, dignity, and financial security, feeling valued and free from isolation.

“Aging is a beautiful chapter of life. Let us make it Timeless together.”
